How to Enter Data
Quick guide to recording daily business transactions
1
Record a Sale
Go to Dashboard or Sales → click + Record Sale → select product, enter quantity, rate, buyer name and payment mode → click Save Sale. The transaction appears in the Sales table instantly.
2
Record a Purchase
Go to Purchases → click + New Purchase → select supplier, product, enter quantity, rate, and how much was paid now → click Save Purchase. Partial payments are tracked as credit dues.
3
Record an Expense
Go to Expenses → click + New Expense → choose category (Labour, Transport, Rent, etc.), add description, amount and payment mode → click Save Expense. This deducts from net profit in Reports.
4
View Reports & Analytics
Use the period selector (Today / 7 Days / 30 Days) in the header to filter all data. Go to Reports for the full Profit & Loss statement, revenue trends, and product-level margin analysis.
5
Monitor Inventory
Go to Inventory to see current stock levels. Items below reorder level show an amber warning. Out-of-stock items show a red alert on the dashboard banner.
